Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

EESI Elevators Inc. ("EESI", "we", "us") operates the EESI Operations Console (the "App") for use by its employees, technicians, and authorized representatives.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: full name, employee ID, email, phone, address, team, and area of assignment.
  • Work records: daily time records, service and delivery reports, PMS checklists, notes, recommendations, and leave requests.
  • Communications: in-app chat messages and attachments.
  • Location: only when you press "Start sharing location". You can stop at any time.
  • Media: profile and cover photos, signature images, and photos you attach to reports.

2. Purpose

We process this data solely to operate the App: authenticating users, recording work activities, assigning schedules, coordinating field operations, and reviewing leave requests.

3. Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, the performance of your employment or contractual relationship with EESI, and EESI's legitimate business interests in operating its services.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row Level Security enforced. All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Access is limited to authorized EESI administrators.

5. Retention

Account data is retained while you are an authorized user. Chat messages, location history, and reports are retained for as long as required by EESI's operational and legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and only to the extent necessary to operate the App.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to rectify, to erase or block, to damages, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer.
